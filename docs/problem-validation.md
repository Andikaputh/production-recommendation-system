# Problem Validation Notes

RASA tests whether students starting college or moving into boarding accommodation need help assembling several essentials within one budget. The target segment, single-merchant B2B2C distribution, merchant willingness to pay, and benefit over existing store curation remain unvalidated hypotheses.

Current evidence covers software engineering and evaluation on synthetic data. Ranking, feasibility, cheapest-item and fixed-curation baselines, and ablation do not establish real preferences, conversion, or revenue. Clicks, basket acceptance, and locally prepared summaries are not purchases.

Use the [business validation pack](pilot/business-validation.md) for neutral interviews, manual/curated/RASA usability tasks, a pilot proposal, and continue/stop criteria. The [experiment plan](pilot/measurement.md) separates formative research from powered user experiments. Indonesian fieldwork materials are retained for local participants. No interviews, market size, partners, transactions, or testimonials are fabricated.

See the [evaluation investigation](model/evaluation-audit.md) before interpreting the synchronized results below.
